Our Mission

Through weekly 1-on-1 English lessons, we provide learning support for children with rare diseases, helping them learn through stories and interaction.

01

1-on-1 Personalized Teaching

Each volunteer teacher is matched with one student. Teaching content is customized based on the child's situation and progress, using Lexile English APP or designated materials.

02

Flexible Interactive Courses

45-minute sessions once a week via Tencent Meeting. Incorporating game interactions, storytelling, phonics practice, and other innovative teaching methods.

03

Official Certificate

Complete one term and meet assessment requirements to receive an official volunteer service certificate from Shanghai CORD Rare Disease Organization.
